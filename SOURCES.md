# Sources — Plant Resources (decks + Flow Meter Selector)

Where the information in this folder's root decks (*Absorbers*, *Condensers*, *Cooling Towers*,
*Flow Meters*, *Process Safety Information*, *Storage Vessels*) and the Flow Meter Selector tool
comes from. Subprojects (`Materials of Construction`, `P&ID`) keep their own SOURCES.md.
Topic-level, not number-by-number: the aim is to show nothing was invented, and to point a reader to
the right book and section if they want to dig.

**Tiers**
- **Library** — reference textbook in `Downloads\ChemE\Textbooks (Level 1)\` (used across projects)
- **Project PDF** — standard or excerpt held in this folder
- **Web** — public page, URL given
- **Print only** — book cited from a paper copy; no digital copy for page lookup
- **Own work** — derived, fitted or chosen in this project; the basis is stated

Copyrighted PDFs are cited here but never published.

> **Encyclopedia.** Rows marked *Condensed Encyclopedia* cite Cheremisinoff, N.P., *Chemical Engineer's
> Condensed Encyclopedia of Process Equipment*, Gulf Publishing, 2000 (ix + 387 pp., ISBN 0-88415-144-1).
> It is the source for the *Absorbers*, *Condensers*, *Cooling Towers*, *Flow Meters* and *Storage
> Vessels* decks. No digital copy is held, so these rows are Print only.
